Simple now, self-sufficient later.

Currently authorized to work in the US under F-1 STEM OPT through October 11, 2027. That means no H-1B lottery, no immediate visa petition, and no long-term immigration commitment required to hire now. What's needed near-term is two standard, low-friction compliance steps:

STEP 1

E-Verify enrollment

A free federal system. The employer creates an account, signs the standard memorandum of understanding, and completes a ~1-hour tutorial. No government fee, no immigration filing, just standard HR onboarding.

STEP 2

Form I-983 training plan

Not a petition to USCIS. A signed document confirming the role relates to my degree and the company provides structured training. I'd coordinate this with my university's international office and handle the reporting calendar.

Bottom line for now: the employer's ask is enrolling in E-Verify and signing one training-plan form, then employing me under existing authorization already in place through Oct 2027.

ON TITLE
Head of Software Engineering, Product & AI
Backup: Technical Lead – Software Engineering, Product & AI
O-1A weighs heavily on proving a "critical or essential role." Given the actual scope here, sole technical owner of the database, API layer, infrastructure, and analysis, this title better documents that standard than a narrower one would, and strengthens the case alongside an org chart and role description.

The path after that is the O-1A visa, for individuals with extraordinary ability, built on demonstrated impact rather than a random lottery. As a founding/lead technical hire, the role naturally creates the kind of documented work (architecture decisions, infrastructure ownership, measurable impact) that supports a strong O-1A case, filed well before STEM OPT expires.
